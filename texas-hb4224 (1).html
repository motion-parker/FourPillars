Regulatory Body

Contacting BHEC

Visit BHEC Website

BHEC — the Texas Behavioral Health Executive Council — regulates licensed mental health professionals across the state of Texas.

If you have questions about licensure or professional standards, you may contact the Texas State Board of Examiners of Professional Counselors, a division of BHEC, directly.

If you would like to file a consumer complaint against a business in the state of Texas, please contact the Texas Attorney General's Office. You can file a complaint online or learn more about your consumer rights.

Your Rights

Requesting Mental Health Records

You have the right to request a copy of your mental health records. Records will be provided in accordance with Texas law and applicable privacy regulations.

Requests may be submitted via mail, in writing, or through the messaging feature of your secure client portal.

Mail Records Requests To Four Pillars Counseling, PLLC
PO Box 5145
Bella Vista, AR 72714

Important Notice

All requests must unambiguously state that the purpose is to request delivery of your mental health records. Sign your ROI first.

A signed Release of Information (ROI) is required before records are provided.

Records Policy Details

What to Know Before You Request

01

Copy Fees

A fee of $2.00 per page of client records will be assessed to cover copy and printing charges.

02

Delivery Time

Please submit your records request with plenty of advance notice. Allow 2 weeks for delivery after your request has been processed.

03

Request Format

Requests must clearly state their purpose and must not mix other questions or unrelated matters. One purpose per request, please.

04

Possible Denial

Under certain limited circumstances listed in 45 CFR 164.524(a)(2)–(a)(3), access to all or a portion of the requested PHI may be denied.

A Release of Information (ROI) must be signed prior to receiving any requested records. The ROI must be on file before records are released under any circumstances.

Submission Methods

How to Submit a Request

Via Secure Client Portal

Use the messaging feature within your secure client portal to submit a records request. This is the fastest and most secure method.

In Writing

Submit a written request directly to our office. Clearly state that the purpose of your communication is to request a delivery of mental health records.

Via Mail

Send your written request to: Four Pillars Counseling, PLLC — PO Box 5145, Bella Vista, AR 72714. Allow 2 weeks for processing and delivery.
