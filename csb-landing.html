The Work

What You Can Expect

Compulsive sexual behavior — in some cases conceptualized as sex addiction — isn't just about what you're doing. It's also about what's underlying the behaviors, what you are really trying to satisfy through the behaviors, and the harms that the behavior causes you and others. The goal of therapy in this area isn't to shame you or judge you: it's to help you understand your behavioral patterns and what underlies them — so you can change.

Working through compulsive sexual behavior can be difficult. Sessions are supportive, but direct. You will be asked hard questions. You will be expected to engage honestly in service of your own recovery. You may also be asked to supplement therapy with group therapy and peer-led support groups.

If you are ready to see change in this area of your life, then this is where you belong.
