Every relationship has room to grow.

Whether you’re navigating a rough patch, building something stronger before you commit, or simply looking to communicate better — couples counseling provides the structure and support to get there.

What Couples Counseling Is For

Not just for relationships in crisis

Healthy relationships require work. They are built intentionally, maintained regularly, and repaired when necessary.

Couples counseling at Four Pillars is not reserved for relationships in crisis. Many of the couples who do this work are not on the verge of separation. They are people who value their relationship enough to invest in better communication, deeper understanding, and a stronger foundation for the future.

If you are considering a deeper commitment and want to enter it with clarity and realistic expectations, this work is for you. If you are in a long-term relationship and feel something has quietly drifted — connection, intimacy, the ease of simply being together — this work is for you too.

How the Practice Works

An integrative approach

Four Pillars takes an integrative approach to couples counseling. That means no single therapeutic model is followed rigidly. Instead, the practice draws on a range of evidence-based methods, intentionally selecting and combining approaches based on what your relationship actually needs rather than fitting your situation to a predetermined framework.

In practice, this means sessions may look different depending on where you are and what you’re working on. Some sessions focus on communication by identifying patterns that keep you stuck and developing new ones. Others go deeper, exploring the underlying emotional dynamics that drive surface-level conflict. The common thread is honesty, structure, and a consistent focus on what you are actually trying to build together.

Premarital Counseling

Build a strong foundation before facing the pressures of married life.

Engagement is an exciting time. It is also an important one.

The decisions and conversations that happen before marriage — about finances, family, conflict, expectations, and values — are foundational. Premarital counseling at Four Pillars creates a structured space where couples can build that strong foundation before facing the pressures of married life.
