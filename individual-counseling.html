What Individual Counseling Is For

People come to individual counseling for many reasons — and every one of them is valid.

People come to individual counseling for many different reasons. Some arrive in the middle of a specific crisis — a loss, a transition, a relationship that has fractured. Others come with something quieter and harder to name: a persistent sense that something isn't working, that they are capable of more, or that the way they are moving through life isn't quite right.

No matter what brings a person to therapy, there is nothing weak about asking for help. There is, however, a cost to waiting — paid quietly, over time, in ways that are easy to overlook until they are hard to ignore.

How the Work Is Done

Tailored to each client

Individual counseling at Four Pillars is tailored to each client. Some clients benefit most from structured, goal-oriented work — identifying specific patterns, developing concrete skills, and tracking measurable progress. Others need something more exploratory — space to examine their history, understand their emotional landscape, and discover what they actually want.

The practice works with each client to determine which approach fits and adjusts as the work evolves. What remains constant is the commitment to honesty and a focus on durable change.

Counseling for Attorneys

A practice that speaks your professional language.

The legal profession is demanding in ways that are difficult to fully appreciate from the outside. Long hours, high stakes, adversarial dynamics, and the weight of other people's most serious problems — these are not ordinary workplace stressors. They are chronic, cumulative, and often invisible to those outside the profession.

Four Pillars brings something uncommon to the therapeutic space: an owner and counselor who practiced law, understands the profession's demands, and has lived in its culture.

If the pressures of the legal profession are impacting your wellbeing, harming your relationships, or threatening your fitness to practice, Four Pillars speaks your professional language and stands ready to help.

The practice has built a dedicated resource specifically for attorneys.
